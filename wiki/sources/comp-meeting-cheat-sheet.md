## Summary

On-screen reference for a 15-minute comp discussion with managers. Structured as a 7-step conversation flow — anchor positive, present data (CPI-adjusted real gain dropped from ~1.5–1.8pp to 0.8pp; bonus fell from ~9% of base to ~1.8%), state the emotional impact plainly, ask management to escalate, then listen.

## Key Takeaways

- Lead with gratitude — this isn't a complaint about feedback
- Two hard data points: real gain less than half of prior years, bonus ~78% cut in percentage terms
- Pause after each data point to let it land
- Say "affecting motivation" plainly, then stop talking
- Ask them to take it to their management — especially the bonus (bigger outlier)
- Hand it back: "how does that land?" — then listen
- Commit to sending numbers in writing after the call
