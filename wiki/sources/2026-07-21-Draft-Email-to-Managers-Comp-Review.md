**Subject: Following up on my increase/bonus — some numbers worth a look**

Hi both,

Thanks again for the feedback in our discussion — it means a lot to hear it was a really good year, genuinely.

I've had a chance to sit with the numbers since, and wanted to share what I found, because I think it's worth a second look before this is finalised.

I pulled my salary and bonus history off my last four IRP5s and checked it against Stats SA's CPI figures:

| Year | SA CPI | My Increase | Real Gain | Bonus (% of base) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2023/24 | 6.08% | 7.70% | +1.62 pp | 9.37% |
| 2024/25 | 4.36% | 5.80% | +1.44 pp | 8.80% |
| 2025/26 | 3.22% | 5.02% | +1.80 pp | 8.74% |
| 2026/27 (proposed) | ~3.2% | 4.00% | **+0.80 pp** | **1.81%** |

Two things stand out to me:

1. The real, inflation-adjusted gain on the proposed increase is less than half of what it was last year — 0.80 percentage points versus 1.80.
2. My bonus has consistently sat at 8.7–9.4% of base salary for four years straight. The proposed R19,000 is 1.81% of base — roughly a 78% cut, not just a smaller number but a completely different scale from every prior year.

Given the feedback I received on the year itself, this outcome doesn't line up with the pattern, and honestly it's landed harder than I expected. I'd really appreciate it if you could take this back to your management and see whether there's room to close that gap — particularly on the bonus, since that's the bigger outlier.

Happy to talk through the numbers in person if that's easier. Thanks for hearing me out on this.

Marthinus
